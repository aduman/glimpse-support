Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Glimpse handles information when you use the app. We aim to keep document workflows private and straightforward.

Documents and local data

Glimpse opens files and folders you choose. Your original documents remain in the locations you manage. We do not operate a cloud document storage service for Glimpse.

Preferences, recent-file access bookmarks and workspace recovery copies are stored in app data on your Mac. Recovery copies may contain unsaved document text. Local data remains until it is removed through the app’s document and recovery lifecycle or deleted from your Mac. Contact support for help removing Glimpse data without deleting your original documents.

Remote images and links

A document may reference remote images. Loading them connects to their hosts and can disclose your IP address and requested resource. Opening a link uses the destination’s privacy policy.

On-device assistance

On supported Macs, optional document assistance uses Apple’s on-device capabilities. Glimpse does not send your document text to a cloud language service for these features. Compatible hardware, macOS and Apple Intelligence availability are required.

Optional crash diagnostics in Glimpse 2.0

In Glimpse 2.0, sharing crash diagnostics is off until you enable it in Settings. Sentry processes reports to help us diagnose crashes. You can turn sharing off at any time. The app stops future capture, cancels pending delivery and removes its queued diagnostic data before reporting can resume. Turning sharing off does not delete reports already received.

Reports contain limited technical crash information, including the app version, crash type, technical stack addresses and debug identifiers. Before sending events, Glimpse removes document text, file paths, user identifiers, arbitrary messages and activity breadcrumbs. Sentry receives the network connection and may retain its IP address under the current server settings. We do not use diagnostics for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Earlier versions may collect broader crash and performance diagnostics under different defaults and controls. Update to Glimpse 2.0 to use the explicit consent controls described above.

Retention and deletion

Our Sentry organization stores data in the European Union. Under the current Developer plan, new error-event data has a 30-day retention period. This is separate from aggregate issue history and service audit records. Backups expire under Sentry’s backup schedule; removal does not mean immediate deletion from every backup.

Contact membrain.app@gmail.com for a privacy or deletion request. The app version and approximate report date can help us locate a report. Do not send credentials or document contents. We assess what records can be identified and use supported deletion tools; deleting an error can require deleting its whole grouped issue. Glimpse 2.0 does not attach a user account identifier to crash events. We update this policy if retention for new reports changes.

Purchases and subscriptions

Apple processes App Store purchases, eligible subscription trials, renewal, cancellation and purchase restoration. We do not receive your full payment card details. Apple retains its purchase records under its own privacy policy. Older Glimpse releases may also retain a local evaluation date.

Your choices and contact

Choose which documents to open, control diagnostic sharing in Settings, and manage subscriptions through your Apple account. We do not sell personal information. Contact membrain.app@gmail.com with privacy questions or requests.
